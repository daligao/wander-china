Why it's different

Not another "AI itinerary generator"

Most AI trip planners hallucinate wrong hours and send you to closed museums. Here's what makes ours actually reliable.

🚫

Closed-day awareness

The system knows which museums close on Mondays, which temples need advance booking, and which spots are "night only." Conflicts are automatically resolved — not silently ignored.

🗺️

Route optimization

A real travel-time matrix clusters nearby spots together. Day attractions go in the morning, night spots in the evening. You never zig-zag across a city because the AI didn't look at a map.

📚

Human-verified facts

Every ticket price, opening hour, and metro stop is manually checked — not generated. AI shapes the schedule; the facts underneath are real and current.

🈚

Designed for "no Chinese"

Every attraction has English-friendliness notes, WeChat booking guides, and local tips specifically for visitors who don't read Chinese characters.

🧩

Plan like a game

Drag spots onto a timeline, watch timing and budget update live. You can override anything the AI suggests — it's a starting point, not a locked itinerary.

💸

Real budget picture

Ticket costs add up automatically as you plan. Add your flight origin and travel style for a full trip cost estimate — flights, hotels, food, and activities included.
