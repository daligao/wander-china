Learn Chinese Antonyms Through Play

This free matching game teaches you Chinese opposite words (反义词, fǎnyìcí) through an interactive click-to-match format. Every tile shows the Chinese character, its pinyin romanization, and an English translation — making it ideal for beginners and intermediate learners alike.

The game covers 380 antonym pairs across three difficulty levels: single characters (Easy), two-character compound words (Medium), and advanced compounds (Hard). Each round presents 4 random pairs as 8 tiles. Match all pairs to advance, earn combo multipliers for consecutive correct matches, and use the hint button when stuck.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Chinese antonym (反义词)?
A 反义词 (fǎnyìcí) is a word that means the opposite of another word. For example, 大 (dà, big) and 小 (xiǎo, small), or 冷 (lěng, cold) and 热 (rè, hot). Learning antonym pairs is one of the most effective ways to double your vocabulary — every new word teaches you two concepts at once.
How do I play the Chinese antonyms matching game?
Click any tile to select it (it turns purple), then click its opposite to make a match. A correct match turns both tiles green and awards points — consecutive correct matches earn a combo multiplier (up to ×8). A wrong match shakes the tiles red and resets your combo. Use the 💡 Hint button (−20 pts) to highlight a matching pair, or Skip to move to the next round.
What Chinese antonym pairs does the game include?
The game includes 380 carefully curated pairs sourced from a Chinese antonym dictionary. The Easy level focuses on common single characters like 黑/白 (black/white), 大/小 (big/small), 冷/热 (cold/hot). The Medium and Hard levels introduce compound words like 安全/危险 (safe/dangerous), 勤劳/懒惰 (hardworking/lazy), and 坚定/动摇 (resolute/waver).
Is this game good for HSK preparation?
Yes. The Easy level covers vocabulary from HSK 1–2, Medium covers HSK 3–4, and Hard covers HSK 4–6. Studying antonyms is especially effective for the HSK reading and vocabulary sections, where questions often test your ability to distinguish between similar or opposite words. Pair this game with our Mandarin Flashcards tool for comprehensive HSK prep.
Does the game show pinyin?
Yes — every tile displays the Chinese character, its pinyin (phonetic romanization with tone marks), and an English translation. You can toggle English hints on or off during the game using the checkbox in the top bar. Pinyin is always visible to help with pronunciation.
Is this game free? Does it require an account?
Completely free, no account, no login, no ads. The game runs entirely in your browser — no data is sent to any server. Works on desktop and mobile.
