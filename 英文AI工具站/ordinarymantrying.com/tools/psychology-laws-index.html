40 Psychology Laws

Complete blog series explaining 40 psychology laws with origin stories, real-world examples, and practical applications.

What are these? Psychology laws are patterns in human behavior and decision-making that repeat across contexts. They're not laws of physics — they're recurring truths about how minds work.

Why this matters: Each law is a lens for understanding decisions you make (or see others make) every day. Recognizing them helps you avoid traps and make better choices.

How to use this: Pick a law that interests you, read the full article with examples, then try the Mind Traps quiz to test your understanding.
