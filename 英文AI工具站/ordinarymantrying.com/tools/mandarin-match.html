Frequently Asked Questions

Is Mandarin Match free?

Yes — completely free. No account, no payment, no ads. Open the page and start playing immediately.

What Chinese words does it cover?

1743 Mandarin words spanning everyday vocabulary, HSK 1–4 core words, verbs, adjectives, nouns, and common phrases. Each tile shows the Chinese character plus pinyin so you learn pronunciation while matching.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Once the page loads, it works without an internet connection. Progress is saved in your browser's LocalStorage so you can resume from where you left off — even after closing the tab.

How does the matching game work?

Each level shows 8 Chinese tiles and 8 English tiles (16 total). Tap a Chinese character, then tap its English meaning. A correct match removes both tiles and builds your combo streak. Clear all 8 pairs to advance to the next level.

Is it good for beginners learning Chinese?

Yes. The game starts with basic vocabulary and gradually introduces more complex words. Pinyin is always visible so you can learn pronunciation as you go. It works best alongside structured lessons — use it to reinforce and test vocabulary you're studying.

What are the badges and achievements?

Badges unlock as you hit milestones — first match, first level complete, 10-level streak, 100 words learned, and more. All badge progress is saved locally so you can collect them over multiple sessions.
