Key Takeaways

What the Numbers Actually Mean

Street food is unbeatable value
A basic restaurant meal (¥25, ~$3.50) is 5.8× cheaper than the US equivalent. Eating local in China on ¥300/month is entirely realistic.
Property in Tier-1 cities is expensive
Beijing's per-m² price (¥50,888) exceeds the US city-center average (¥32,488). Home ownership is harder in Shanghai/Beijing than in most US cities.
The salary gap explains everything
China's average salary (¥6,711/mo) is 4.8× lower than the US (¥32,381/mo). Most things ARE cheaper — but so is income. The real gap is in housing and cars.
Rent outside Tier-1 is very affordable
A single room in Chengdu averages ¥643/month ($89). In Baoding (4th largest city in Hebei), it's ¥233/month. Tier-2/3 cities offer striking value.
