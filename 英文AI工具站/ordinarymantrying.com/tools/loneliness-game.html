Why this test exists

Do you often feel like you want to reconnect with friends but don't know how to start? The Loneliness Test is the world's most creative icebreaker tool designed to help you overcome social awkwardness. Instead of stressing about how to ask a friend out, this game turns the invitation into a fun, low-pressure challenge.

Is this a real psychology test?

Not at all! This is purely an entertainment and social tool. It's not a diagnostic test. If you find yourself struggling with social interactions, think of this game as a gentle nudge to reach out. It's the perfect way to reconnect with friends, grab a coffee, or plan a movie night without the fear of rejection.

How to use this tool to ask friends out

It's simple: Play the game, find your level, and use the automatically generated invitation to text a friend. It takes the "awkward" out of "Can we hang out?" and replaces it with a fun conversation starter.
