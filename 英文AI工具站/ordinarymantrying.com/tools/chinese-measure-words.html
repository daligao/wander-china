What Are Chinese Measure Words (量词)?

In Mandarin, you can't say "three book" — you need a measure word (量词 liàngcí): 三本书 (three books). Every noun has one or more associated measure words. This game teaches you to sort nouns by their correct measure word, across 8 progressively harder levels.

  • What is the most common Chinese measure word?
    个 (gè) is the most versatile. It works for people (一个人), fruits (一个苹果), and most nouns when you're unsure of the specific measure word.
  • What's the difference between 条 tiáo and 根 gēn?
    Both describe long thin objects. 条 (tiáo) is for flexible things — fish, snakes, rivers, roads. 根 (gēn) is for rigid stick-like things — bananas, chopsticks, candles.
  • What measure word is used for books?
    本 (běn): 一本书 (yī běn shū). Also for magazines (杂志) and notebooks (笔记本).
  • Are measure words tested on HSK?
    Yes — throughout HSK 1–4. 个, 张, 条, 只, 本 appear at HSK 1–2; 首, 篇, 座, 封 at HSK 3–4. Level 7–8 of this game covers advanced ones.
