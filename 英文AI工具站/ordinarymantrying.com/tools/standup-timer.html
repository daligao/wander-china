◈ HOW THIS GAME WORKS
💰
Earn While You Work
Your net worth grows at +$2.00/min while in Focus Mode. The longer you stay focused, the more you earn — but health slowly drains at −1.5/min.
⏱
Auto-Break at Your Timer
When the ring empties, the game auto-triggers a break and plays an alert. You can also break early — at a cost of 1% of your net worth as a "health maintenance fee."
⚡
60-Min Hard Kill
Miss your break and sit for a full 60 minutes? Instant game over. All assets seized for the ICU bill. This mirrors real biology: sudden health collapse from chronic sitting.
📈
The $9,000 Wealth Ceiling
Work 45 min → earn $90. Rest costs 1% of worth. When worth = $9,000, the 1% fee equals $90 — your profit. Single-handedly proves you can't escape the time-for-money trap.
🪑
Invest to Break the Ceiling
Buy the Ergonomic Chair ($2K) to cut health drain by 33%. Buy the Standing Desk ($5K) to halve the rest fee and raise your ceiling to $18,000.
❤️
Why This Matters IRL
Prolonged sitting compresses pelvic blood flow, linked to prostate issues, back pain, and metabolic risk. Standing up every 45 min is one of the highest-ROI health habits.
